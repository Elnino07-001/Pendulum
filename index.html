Pendulum Lab

Two simulations of the simple pendulum. The first compares the exact nonlinear motion with the period formula T = 4√(L/g)·K(sin(θ₀/2)). The second is a virtual lab bench for timing swings by hand and analysing the data.

Ideal pendulum & elliptic integral

RK4 simulation of θ̈ = −(g/L) sin θ beside the small-angle model, with live period measurement and an amplitude sweep against 2K(k)/π.

Experiment Bench

A non-ideal pendulum with a finite bob, adjustable air drag and friction, and a stopwatch you operate yourself. Record trials and fit T² vs L, ln T vs ln L, T vs θ₀², and more.
